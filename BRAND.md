**What Tenorworth is**

The name does real work for you: "tenor" is the steady course a thing holds (the tenor of an agreement, of a conversation), and "worth" is measurable value. Together it reads as *steady, measurable value* — exactly the promise a credit union COO or a healthcare system's VP of Ops wants to hear about AI, and the opposite of the hype they're tired of.

**One-liner:** Tenorworth is an AI implementation consultancy that puts working AI systems inside regulated and operations-heavy businesses — safely, on a fixed scope, with a measured return.

**Positioning (the four sentences you'll repeat everywhere):**
- *Who:* Small and mid-market businesses in Southern California where trust and compliance matter — community healthcare, credit unions and RIAs, law firms, life sciences, and hospitality operators.
- *What:* Not strategy decks. Deployed agents, RAG on their own documents, and workflow automation that their staff actually use.
- *How:* Fixed-scope engagements — a two-week AI Readiness Roadmap, a 4–6 week Pilot on their real data, and a Fractional AI Architect retainer — delivered by someone who has built this inside JPMC, Scotiabank, DaVita, and PwC.
- *Why us:* 17 years in regulated environments means the governance, audit trail, and data-boundary questions get answered before the demo, not after.

**Tagline options:** "AI, deployed with care." / "Working AI for regulated business." / "Steady AI. Measurable worth." (the last one leans on the name; the first is the safest for a business card.)

**Brand attributes to hold constant:** composed, precise, senior, quietly confident. Never "futuristic," never neon, no robots, no brains, no circuit boards. Think a good boutique law firm or a Swiss engineering firm, not a SaaS startup. Legal entity stays TravAlarm, Inc.; Tenorworth is the practice brand (dba).

**Visual direction**
- Palette: deep navy or ink (#141B2D), warm off-white (#F4F1EA), one accent — a muted brass (#B8985A) or a deep teal (#1F6F78). Pick one accent and stick with it.
- Type: a humanist serif for the wordmark (Fraunces, Source Serif, or Instrument Serif), a clean grotesk for everything else (Inter or Geist).
- Mark concept: a single horizontal "tenor line" — a steady baseline with one measured rise, suggesting both a steady course and a return curve. Simple enough to work as a 16px favicon.

**Logo prompt** (for GPT-image, Midjourney, or Ideogram — generate the mark only; set the wordmark in real type yourself, since image models mangle letters):

> Minimalist logo mark for "Tenorworth," a senior AI consultancy serving regulated businesses. A single confident horizontal line that holds steady, then rises once in a clean measured curve, ending in a small solid circle — evoking a steady course and measurable growth. Geometric, balanced, engineered feel; stroke weight consistent; the mark reads clearly at 16px. Flat vector style, no gradients, no 3D, no text. Ink navy (#141B2D) mark on warm off-white (#F4F1EA) background, centered, generous negative space. Aesthetic references: Swiss modernist identity, boutique law firm, precision instruments. Avoid: robots, brains, circuits, sparkles, glowing effects, sans-serif letters embedded in the mark.

Ask for 4–6 variations, then request the winner as "same mark, monochrome, high contrast, isolated on white, vector-clean" for tracing in Illustrator or Figma.

**Wordmark spec** (do this in Figma, not an image model): "Tenorworth" set in Fraunces at medium weight, slightly tightened tracking, all lowercase or Title Case (test both), mark to the left with clear space equal to the height of the "T." Secondary lockup: mark above the word, centered, for the favicon/app icon.
